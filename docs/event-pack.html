2 · What each table is asked

In order, exactly as the phone shows it. Every typed field is optional; only the choices are required.

Screen 1 · the lens

How do you imagine your future cognitive city?

Four cities. The pick is hidden analysis — it never appears by name on the wall.

The garden city

Nature first, buildings second. There is no boundary: the office IS the parkland, low and half-buried in it, and you are never indoors for long.

The vertical city

One enormous structure holds everything: work, homes, food, transit. You need never go outside.

The self-sufficient city

It makes its own energy, food and water, and shows you doing it. Dense and urban — solar skins, edible facades, pipes and pumps left on display.

Neo Retro

The more we walk into the future, the more we want the past. Deco geometry, brass, streamlined curves — a tomorrow built out of yesterday.

Also on this screen: No future fits us — skip. A skipped lens still draws — the house style supplies what the lens would have.
